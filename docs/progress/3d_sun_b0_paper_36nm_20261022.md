## Paper-to-CMP comparisons

Original paper: Sun, Baac, Shin, *Micromachines* 13, 1476 (2022), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.3390/mi13091476). Table 1, row **DBCAT**; 36 nm black-diamond baseline, 24 nm blue triangles, 48 nm magenta pentagons. Paper plots `Id–Vg: Vd=1.2 V` and `Id–Vd: Vg=0.1/0.6/1.2 V`. Paper-reported nominal Vth=0.656 V, SS=76 mV/dec, Ion/Ioff=3.4e10, DIBL=23.6 mV/V.

### Id–Vg

CMP high-Vd constant-current Vth≈**0.782709 V**, paper nominal **0.656 V** (indicative +**0.126709 V**, paper drain-bias/extraction equivalence not completely demonstrated); CMP LOWVD SS≈**75.887 mV/dec** (paper nominal **76**), project DIBL≈**21.882 mV/V** (paper nominal **23.6**). The subthreshold steepness and turn-on pattern **qualitatively resemble** the paper baseline. Absolute Vth and strong-on current **do not directly match**.

### Id–Vd

All amplitudes below are native drain total-current magnitude divided by nominal Wfin **0.017 µm**; this is an **illustrative derived current-per-width** and may not equal paper's effective channel-width convention.

| Gate voltage | CMP Vd=1.2 V (A/µm) | Paper visual comparison |
|---|---:|---|
| 0.1 V | 5.295908166897099e-13 | Paper `~10^-5 A/µm` family; many orders greater |

**Critical distinction:** `~` paper values come from **visual reading of raster Table 1**, not tabular original/signed underlying data. They are *not* exact digitized reference or least-squares fit anchors. The overlay delivered to the user has paper's actual Table 1 chart image as background and CMP data mapped to its pixel axes; it demonstrates visible shape/amplitude divergence, not exact paper-point error metrics.

The 0.1/0.6 V Id–Vd deviations are too substantial to claim strong 36-nm absolute replication. The 1.2 V curve has a broadly similar rise but CMP is higher by approximately tens of percent at Vd~1.2 V and shows continued rising. Thus **paper-fit status: partial/qualitative only**.
